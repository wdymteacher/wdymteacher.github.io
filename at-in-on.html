Big idea: AT = a point · ON = a surface / a day · IN = inside a space / a longer period

📌 Remember:

AT

⏰ clock times & moments – at 7 p.m., at noon, at night
📍 a specific point / place – at the bus stop, at home, at work, at a party

IN

⏰ longer periods & parts of the day – in July, in 2026, in the morning
📍 inside a space – in the kitchen, in my bag, in a car, in Brazil

ON

⏰ days & dates – on Monday, on May 5th, on my birthday
📍 a surface / street / the bus – on the table, on Paulista Avenue, on the bus
